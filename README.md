# Adidas Corporate Finance & Capital Structure Analysis

## Overview
This repository contains a comprehensive corporate finance case study on Adidas, evaluating the company's financial strategies, capital structure, competitive positioning, and dividend policies. The analysis compares Adidas against major industry peers (Nike, Puma, Under Armour) to identify financial vulnerabilities and opportunities for growth.

## Key Areas of Analysis
* **Cost of Capital (WACC & CAPM):** Calculated Weighted Average Cost of Capital across varying debt levels to identify the optimal debt-to-capital ratio.
* **Solvency & Liquidity:** Analyzed historical Debt-to-Equity, Net Debt-to-EBITDA, and EBIT Interest Coverage ratios from 2019-2023.
* **Capital Structure Optimization:** Evaluated the trade-offs between debt tax shields and financial distress risks, particularly regarding Adidas's declining intangible asset valuation.
* **Dividend Policy & Share Buybacks:** Assessed the impact of Adidas's cyclical dividend policy compared to the stable payout structures of its peers.

## Key Findings & Recommendations
1. **Optimal Capital Structure:** The analysis determined that Adidas achieves its lowest WACC (7.04%) and maximizes firm value at a **20% debt-to-capital ratio**. 
2. **Current Over-leverage:** Adidas currently operates at a 28.48% ratio (€13.09B in debt). 
3. **Strategic Action:** We recommend a debt reduction of ~€3.89B utilizing operating cash flows and the divestment of non-core assets to enhance long-term financial stability and improve credit ratings (which recently dropped to A- with a negative outlook).
